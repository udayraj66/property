Our Story

Built on craft, defined by trust.

For over a decade, Aurum has been shaping premium residential and commercial spaces — grounded in architecture, honesty and an obsession with the details that matter.

Who We Are

We don't just build properties. We build the places people call home.

Aurum was founded on a simple belief — that real estate should be judged not by square footage, but by how it makes people feel. Every development we undertake begins with that question.

Today, our portfolio spans residential towers, private villas and commercial landmarks across India, each shaped by considered design, honest materials and a deep respect for the communities they become part of.

We work with award-winning architects, sustainability consultants and craftsmen who share our standard for quality — because a building outlives a transaction, and we design for that.
